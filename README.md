# AI-Impact-on-Human-Skills-Behaviour-2025-2075

Interactive dashboard analyzing the long‑term impact of AI adoption on human skills and behaviour (2025–2075).  
Built with **Looker Studio + Excel**, featuring KPI cards, conditional formatting, and visual storytelling to highlight trends in creativity, wellbeing, dependency, and attention span across regions and demographics.

---

## 📌 Objective
- Explore how AI adoption influences human skills and behaviour over time  
- Analyze creativity, wellbeing, dependency, and attention span across regions and demographics  
- Provide insights into trade‑offs between efficiency and originality  
- Present recruiter‑friendly storytelling through interactive visuals  

---

## 📂 Data Sources
- **Dataset:** AI adoption & human skills metrics (synthetic projections)  
- **Timeline:** 2025 – 2075  
- **Domain:** Behavioural Analytics  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Looker Studio  
- Microsoft Excel  
- Conditional Formatting  

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation
- Cleaned and standardized data formats  
- Created calculated fields for dependency, creativity, wellbeing, and attention span  
- Applied conditional formatting thresholds for intuitive storytelling  
- Built interactive year slider for trend analysis  

---

## 📐 Data Modelling
- Star schema design with clear relationships  
- Fact table: AI Impact Statistics  
- Dimension tables: Calendar, Region, Demographics  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for quick highlights  
- Bar charts for regional comparisons  
- Line chart for dependency trend (2025–2075)  
- Donut chart for attention span by demographic group  
- Scatter plot showing dependency

## 📈 Key Insights
🌍 Regional Usage: Oceania records the highest weekly AI usage hours (24.8 hrs), slightly ahead of North America and South Asia, showing stronger adoption in these regions.

📈 Dependency Growth: AI dependency rises steadily from 2025 to 2075, indicating increasing reliance on AI tools over time.

🎨 Creativity Trade‑off: A clear negative correlation exists between dependency and creativity — higher reliance on AI links to lower creativity scores.

💚 Wellbeing Stability: Mental wellbeing scores remain relatively stable (~62–63), though slight regional differences suggest cultural or contextual influences.

👥 Demographic Differences: Younger demographics (18–25) show shorter attention spans compared to older groups, highlighting generational differences in how AI impacts focus.

🧠 Critical Thinking & Proficiency: Despite rising dependency, critical thinking and AI proficiency scores remain balanced (~63 and ~52 respectively), suggesting adaptation rather than decline.

---

## 📌 Conclusion
The dashboard reveals that while AI adoption is widespread and growing, it comes with trade‑offs in creativity and attention span. Regional and demographic variations highlight the importance of context in understanding AI’s long‑term impact on human behaviour.
